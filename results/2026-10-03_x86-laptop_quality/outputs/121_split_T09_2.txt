  <section id="features">
    <h2>What it does</h2>
    <div class="feat">
      <article class="rise"><h3>One expression language</h3><p>Filter on any field with <code>==</code>, <code>~</code>, <code>&gt;</code> and boolean logic. No jq escaping, no awk gymnastics, and errors point at the exact character that broke.</p></article>
      <article class="rise"><h3>Live tail</h3><p>Follows files, stdin and sockets without buffering delays.</p></article>
      <article class="rise"><h3>Mixed formats</h3><p>JSON, logfmt and plain text are detected per line, so messy pipelines just work.</p></article>
      <article class="rise"><h3>Fast by default</h3><p>Streaming parser, zero allocations on the hot path, and all cores used on big files. A 10 GB archive scans in seconds, not minutes.</p></article>
      <article class="rise"><h3>Pipe-friendly output</h3><p>Emit <code>--json</code>, <code>--csv</code> or a custom <code>--fmt</code> template.</p></article>
      <article class="rise"><h3>Local only</h3><p>No telemetry, no network calls, ever.</p></article>
    </div>
