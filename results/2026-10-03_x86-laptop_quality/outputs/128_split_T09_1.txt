  <section id="features">
    <h2>Features</h2>
    <div>
      <div class="feat reveal"><span>01</span><h3>A query language you can remember</h3><p>Write <code>level>=warn and user:42</code> instead of nested <code>jq</code> filters. Autocomplete for fields ships for bash, zsh and fish.</p></div>
      <div class="feat reveal"><span>02</span><h3>Fast on big files</h3><p>Memory-mapped, parallel parsing scans a 10 GB file in seconds and never loads it whole.</p></div>
      <div class="feat reveal"><span>03</span><h3>Group, count, percentile</h3><p>Pipe results into <code>group</code>, <code>top</code> or <code>p95</code> for quick answers without leaving the shell.</p></div>
      <div class="feat reveal"><span>04</span><h3>Live tail with context</h3><p>Follow a stream with <code>--live</code> and keep the surrounding lines of every match, like <code>grep -C</code> for events.</p></div>
      <div class="feat reveal"><span>05</span><h3>Scriptable output</h3><p>Emit JSON, CSV or colored text. Exit codes are stable, so it behaves in CI.</p></div>
    </div>
