      <div><h2>What it does</h2><p class="sub">Four things, done without ceremony.</p></div>
      <ol class="feat">
        <li><div><h3>Pipe-first query language</h3><p>Chain stages with <code>|</code>: filter, project, group, aggregate. If you know SQL's WHERE, you already know most of it.</p></div></li>
        <li><div><h3>Reads what you already have</h3><p>JSON, logfmt, and plain text with regex capture groups. Gzip and zstd files open directly; format is auto-detected per line.</p></div></li>
        <li><div><h3>Fast on one core</h3><p>Streaming parser scans about 1.2 GB/s on a laptop. Memory stays flat, so a 40 GB archive costs the same RAM as a 40 KB one.</p></div></li>
        <li><div><h3>Live tail with context</h3><p><code>sift -f</code> follows files or stdin, re-evaluates aggregates every second, and keeps surrounding lines around each match.</p></div></li>
      </ol>
