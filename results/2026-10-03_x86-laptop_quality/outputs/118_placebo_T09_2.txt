      <h2>What it does</h2>
      <ul class="features">
        <li><h3><span>01</span>Plain-English filters</h3><p>Write <code>where user is "ana" and status &gt;= 500</code>. Types are inferred from the data, not declared.</p></li>
        <li><h3><span>02</span>Any format, no config</h3><p>JSON, logfmt, and common access-log layouts are detected per line, even when a file mixes them.</p></li>
        <li><h3><span>03</span>Fast on big files</h3><p>Memory-mapped, parallel scanning. Half a million lines in tens of milliseconds on a laptop.</p></li>
        <li><h3><span>04</span>Follow live sources</h3><p>Tail files, stdin, or <code>--k8s deploy/api</code> and keep your filter running as lines arrive.</p></li>
        <li><h3><span>05</span>Readable output</h3><p>Fields are aligned and colored on a TTY. Pipe it elsewhere and you get clean JSON lines instead.</p></li>
        <li><h3><span>06</span>Scriptable</h3><p>Stable exit codes and <code>--count</code> make it a dependable step in CI and alerting scripts.</p></li>
      </ul>
