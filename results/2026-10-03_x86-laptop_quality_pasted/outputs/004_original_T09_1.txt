  <section id="features">
    <h2>What weft does</h2>
    <p class="lead">One binary, no daemon, no account. It reads the files already in your repository.</p>
    <div class="grid">
      <article class="cell w t rv"><h3>Diff across environments</h3><p>Compare staging against production and see which keys are missing, extra or changed. Values are masked unless you pass --reveal.</p>
<pre><code>$ weft diff staging production
+ STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET   missing in production
~ DATABASE_POOL_SIZE      differs (values masked)</code></pre></article>
      <article class="cell rv"><h3>Merge without loss</h3><p>Existing values always win. New keys are added with empty placeholders you can fill in.</p></article>
      <article class="cell rv"><h3>Schema checks in CI</h3><p>Validate every file against a .env.schema and fail the build when a required key drifts.</p></article>
      <article class="cell w rv"><h3>Secret-aware pre-commit hook</h3><p>weft hook install blocks real secrets from landing in .env.example, and rewrites them to safe placeholders when you ask it to.</p></article>
    </div>
  </section>
