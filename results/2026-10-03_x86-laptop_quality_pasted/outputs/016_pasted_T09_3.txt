    <h2>Match code the way the compiler reads it.</h2>
    <p>Write a pattern like <code>fetch($URL, $OPTS)</code> and Shale finds every call, whatever the formatting. Add a replacement and it rewrites them all.</p>
    <p>Every change is shown as a diff first. Nothing touches disk until you pass <code>--apply</code>.</p>
