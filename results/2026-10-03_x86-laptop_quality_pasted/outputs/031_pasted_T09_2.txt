<h1>Rebuild only what changed.</h1>
<p>kiln hashes your inputs, caches every task result, and skips any work it has already done.</p>
<div class="cta"><a class="btn primary" href="#install">Install kiln</a><a class="btn" href="#features">Read the docs</a></div>

command = "cargo build --release"

<span class="c">$ kiln build</span>
build   compiled   14.2s
<span class="c">$ kiln build</span>
build   cached     0.1s</pre>

<section id="features">
<h2 class="rv">What kiln does</h2>
<p class="sub rv">Four things, done properly.</p>
<div class="grid">
<article class="cell c1 rv"><h3>Content-addressed cache</h3><p>Every task is keyed by a hash of its files, environment and command. Same inputs, same result, no rerun.</p></article>
<article class="cell c2 rv"><h3>Parallel by default</h3><p>kiln reads your task graph and runs independent steps on every core you have.</p></article>
<article class="cell c3 rv"><h3>Shared remote cache</h3><p>Point <code>cache.remote</code> at an S3 bucket and your CI reuses what your laptop built.</p></article>
<article class="cell c4 rv"><h3>Plain TOML config</h3><p>One <code>kiln.toml</code> per repository. No plugins to install and no scripting language to learn.</p></article>
</div>
</section>
