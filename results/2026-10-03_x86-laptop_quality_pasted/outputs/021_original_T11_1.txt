<dt>Match volume to nights out</dt><dd>Under 40 L suits one night with a quilt. Go 40 to 60 L for three to five nights, and over 60 L for winter or group gear.</dd>
<dt>Measure your torso</dt><dd>Torso length sets the frame size, not height. A pack that fits carries 20 percent of its load on the hips, not the shoulders.</dd>
